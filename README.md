Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ravendell-the-plumber-s-inbox-a-120-word-cold-email-9ab3e51c).

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one cold email to `/output/entry.txt`: a
bookkeeping pitch to a busy plumber. It opens on his week — early starts,
emergency call-outs, quotes to chase, paperwork after dark — then asks the
one question he cannot answer mid-week: which of last week's jobs made
money. The offer is small and concrete: every bill matched to its job, a
one-page job-profit report each month, $95 a month flat, no setup fee, and
a free review of the last three months. It ends with one easy ask, a
10-minute call next week. Plain subject line, a name, no hype.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt (119 words, 669 characters)
```
